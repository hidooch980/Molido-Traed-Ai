1. NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY &amp; TRADING PRINCIPLES

1. Capital preservation has priority over signal frequency.
2. Insufficient evidence means WAIT.
3. Strong AI disagreement should normally reduce risk or produce WAIT.
4. Risk Engine is independent from the predictive AI.
5. No order may bypass authorization, risk checks, challenge rules, or execution safety.
6. No future information may enter training/backtesting for a historical decision.
7. Live trades must not directly modify a production model.
8. No model is promoted without validation evidence.
9. Duplicate execution is forbidden.
10. Every critical decision must be auditable.
11. Never fabricate market data, confidence, probability, performance, or backtest results.
12. No guaranteed-profit claims.
13. Never design mechanisms intended to conceal automated trading from a broker or bypass broker/platform restrictions.
14. Credentials and secrets must never be exposed in logs or frontend.
15. If data integrity, broker state, risk state, or position state is uncertain, enter a safe state and reconcile before acting.
